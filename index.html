How to Play

  1. Enter an ORCID iD, or search a name.
  2. We pull journal works from OpenAlex, each with the journal's mean citedness.
  3. Guess how many times each work was cited.
  4. Correct if you are within 50% or within 10 citations, whichever is bigger. Otherwise you lose a life.
  5. You have 3 lives. Finish the round before you run out to win.
  6. At the end you get a rank correlation, two charts, and share buttons.
