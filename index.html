How many times was this cited?

  1. Enter the ORCID iD of the researcher you want to play as.
  2. We pull all of their works from OpenAlex, along with each hosting journal's mean citedness (a 2-year, impact-factor-like average) — only works where both the citation count and the journal's mean citedness are known are used.
  3. One work at a time, you'll see its title, journal, and the journal's mean citedness. Guess how many times that specific work was cited.
  4. Within 50% of the real number — or within 10 citations, whichever gives you more room — = 🎉 correct. Otherwise = 💔 you lose a life.
  5. You start with 3 lives. Get through every work in the round before you run out to win!
  6. At the end, see how well journal mean citedness actually predicts a single paper's citations — plus a rank correlation, a plot of your guesses vs. reality, and share buttons.
Quantitative metrics are not a proxy for research quality — read why one number can't describe a journal.
