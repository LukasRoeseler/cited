How to Play

  1. Enter an ORCID iD, or search a name.
  2. We pull journal works from OpenAlex, each with the journal's mean citedness.
  3. Guess how many times each work was cited.
  4. Correct is based on how many times over or under your guess is, not a fixed number. Within about 10% earns a bonus life. Within about 50%, or within 2 citations, nothing happens. Otherwise you lose a life.
  5. You start with 3 lives. Finish the round before you run out to win.
  6. At the end, you get a summary of your results.
Mean citedness is based on citation counts via OpenAlex. It is OpenAlex's 2-year mean citedness, similar to Clarivate's Journal Impact Factor.
Citation counts measure attention, not quality. Read why one number cannot describe a journal.
